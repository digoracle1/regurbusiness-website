What we do

Four things, done properly

  • CAC registration

    Business name registration and limited company incorporation, including MOA/AOA and share allotment paperwork.

  • Compliance

    Tax Identification with the Nigeria Revenue Service, SCUML registration, and trademark filing support.

  • Branding

    Logo and visual identity, company profile, letterhead, business cards and staff IDs.

  • Websites & online presence

    Professional websites, business email, payment and booking setup, and your Google Business Profile.

What we stand for

How we work, and what you can hold us to

  • Honest about outcomes

    Name approvals and trademark registrations are decided by the agencies, not by us. We tell you that upfront instead of promising what we can't control.

  • One flat price

    You know the cost before we start. Government fees are confirmed with you before filing, and nothing is added halfway.

  • Filed properly the first time

    Most delays come from avoidable errors. We check your documents before submission so your filing isn't sent back.

  • A real person on WhatsApp

    No call centres, no ticket numbers. You talk to the people doing the work, from quote to certificate.

  • Your details stay private

    We only share your information with the agencies your filing requires. We publish client work in our gallery only with permission, with details redacted.

  • Compliance, not just registration

    A certificate alone isn't enough for banks and contracts. We handle Tax ID, SCUML and trademarks too.
