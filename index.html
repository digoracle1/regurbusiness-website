    FAQ

    Questions founders ask us

    What documents do I need?

    For a Business Name, we typically need a valid means of ID (NIN slip, international passport, driver's licence or voter's card), a passport photograph, your signature, two or three proposed business names, your business address and a short description of what the business does.

    For a Company (Limited), we need the same details for each director and shareholder, plus the proposed share structure and the company's registered address. Send us a message and we'll give you a simple checklist for your exact case.

    How long does registration take?

    Business name registrations are usually completed within a few working days once your name is approved and documents are complete. Company registrations take a little longer. Final timelines depend on CAC and other agencies' processing, so we'll give you a realistic estimate when we quote and keep you updated on WhatsApp throughout.

    Can I add a website later?

    Yes. You can register now and add a professional website from Digoracle Solutions, our sister company, whenever you're ready. If you already know you'll need one, the Registration + Website bundles are the most cost-effective way to get both.

    What's the difference between Business Name and Company registration?

    A Business Name is the simplest structure. You and the business are legally the same, which suits sole traders and small businesses starting out.

    A Company (Limited) is a separate legal entity with directors and shareholders. It limits your personal liability and is usually expected for government or corporate contracts, investors, and larger bank facilities. Not sure which you need? Ask us and we'll advise honestly.

    Do you handle SCUML or trademark for existing businesses?

    Yes. If you're already registered with CAC, you can order the Compliance Stack (NRS Tax ID, SCUML registration and trademark filing support) or ask us for any of those services individually.
